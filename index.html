    Setting up another kiosk?

    Create your admin login

    Only admins can see the visitor log, review declined visitors, manage cards and change settings. Visitors and staff never need this code.

    • 4 to 8 digits
    • Not an easy pattern like 1234 or 1111
    • Both codes match

    Leave both code fields blank to keep your current admin code.

    Upload your logo

    Use a PNG with a transparent background, with no white box behind the logo, so it looks clean on any screen and badge. This step is optional.

    Where should visitor records be saved?

    Choose what suits your business. You can switch later in Settings, and your existing records come with you.

    Your data stays yours. Records are kept on this device or in your own Google account. This app has no servers of its own, so nothing is ever sent to us.

    Link your people spreadsheet

    Keep your staff and card holders in a Google Sheet. The kiosk reads it, so you can add or remove people from anywhere. This step is optional.

    1. 1
      Create a Google Sheet with these headings in row 1:
      NameCard IDCompanyRoleAdmin
      Open a new Google Sheet

      Only Name is required. Add a Card ID for anyone with an NFC/RFID card, and put "Yes" in the Admin column for admin cards. Everyone listed also appears in the "Who are you visiting?" list.

    2. 2
      Share it so that anyone with the link can view it. Click Share, then under General access choose Anyone with the link and set the role to Viewer.
    3. 3
      Copy and paste the link. Click Copy link, paste it below, then click Test link.
    Anyone with the link can open this sheet, so only include names, card IDs and companies. Don't add personal phone numbers, emails or home addresses.

    Connect your Google Sheet

    Add a small connector script to a Google Sheet that you own. It takes about 5 minutes and you only do it once. Your sheet stays private.

    1. 1
      Create a Google Sheet to hold your visitor log, and give it a name such as "Visitor log".
      Open a new Google Sheet

      The connector adds a People tab (staff and card holders) and a Visits tab (every sign-in) automatically.

    2. 2
      Add the connector script. In the sheet, open Extensions → Apps Script. Delete any code in the editor, paste the script, then click Save.

      The script contains this kiosk's private key:

    3. 3
      Deploy it as a web app. Click Deploy → New deployment, click the gear next to Select type and choose Web app. Set Execute as to Me and Who has access to Anyone, then click Deploy.
    4. 4
      Allow access. When Google asks, choose your account, click Advanced → Go to … (unsafe) → Allow.

      Google shows this warning for any script you add yourself. The script can only open this one spreadsheet and send the alert emails you set up.

    5. 5
      Paste the Web app URL (it ends in /exec) below and test the connection.

    Do you want to ask visitors extra questions?

    Screening questions appear after the sign-in form. Depending on the answers, the kiosk can politely decline a visitor or alert your staff. Card holders skip these questions.

    Quick add

      When a visitor is declined

      With on-device storage, staff alerts appear in the admin panel, and the gear icon shows a dot until someone reviews them. Choose Cloud: Google Sheets storage to also send email alerts.
      Make sure your questions follow local law. Some questions, such as citizenship or health status, are only appropriate where your business is required to ask them, for example under ITAR/EAR export-control rules. Answers and photos are stored with each visit record.
