Set up this iPad

The kiosk runs in Safari, full screen from your Home Screen, and opens even without Wi-Fi once it's added.

Open the kiosk
  1. Add it to the Home ScreenOpen the kiosk, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. Open it from there.
  2. Allow the cameraThe kiosk checks the camera, the badge reader and the internet first, and says how to fix anything.
  3. Answer the setup questionsAbout five minutes.
  4. Lock the iPad to the kioskSettings → Display & Brightness → Auto-Lock → Never. Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access → On, then triple-click the side button in the kiosk.
