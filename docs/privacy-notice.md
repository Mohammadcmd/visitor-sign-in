# Visitor privacy notice (template)

A starting point for the notice to post by the kiosk. Fill in the brackets, delete what doesn't apply, and have it checked against the laws that apply to you (for example state biometric privacy laws, GDPR or CCPA). This template is not legal advice.

---

## How we use your information when you sign in

**Who we are.** [Company name], [address]. Questions: [email or phone].

**What we collect.**
- Your name, company, who you're visiting and why.
- [Your answers to our visitor questions, such as citizenship status for export-control rules.]
- [A photo taken at the kiosk.]
- The time you arrive and leave, and the visitor badge you use.
- [Only if you choose quick entry: a numeric face signature made from your photos, so the kiosk can recognize you next time.]

**Why.** To know who is on site, for safety and security, to let your host know you've arrived, [to meet legal requirements such as U.S. export-control rules,] and to account for everyone in an emergency.

**Where it's kept.** [On the sign-in tablet at our front desk.] [In our company's Google account (Google Sheets and Google Drive).] It is not sold or shared with advertisers.

**Who can see it.** Our front-desk and security staff, and the person you're visiting (their name and your arrival).

**How long we keep it.** [Records are deleted automatically after (period).] [Records are kept for (period), then deleted.]

**Quick entry is your choice.** It is off unless you turn it on. Ask the front desk at any time to turn it off; your face signature is deleted at once.

**Your choices.** You may ask to see or delete your information by contacting [email or phone]. If you prefer not to use the kiosk, please ask the front desk.

---

*Kiosk settings that match each paragraph: Setup → Screening questions (what you ask), Finishing touches → Visitor photos and Quick entry, Finishing touches → Keep visitor records for, Records (on the tablet or Google Sheets).*
