## In short

- You're buying a license to use Visitor Sign-In on each kiosk tablet or phone sign-in QR code you pay for. Each license is for one device or QR code, and it can't be moved to another business.
- The first year of updates and support is included. After that there's a yearly maintenance fee.
- If the yearly fee isn't paid, new sign-ins pause after a {graceDays}-day grace period. Signing out, the roll call, the admin screen and your records keep working, and nothing is deleted.
- Your visitor records stay on your tablet or in your own Google account. {seller} doesn't receive them.
- You're responsible for telling visitors how their information is used and for getting any consent the law requires, especially for photos and face recognition.

## 6. What happens if a fee isn't paid

- **Reminders:** from {warnDays} days before a license's paid-until date, the admin screen reminds your staff.
- **Grace period:** for {graceDays} days after that date, everything keeps working.
- **Then new sign-ins pause** on that device until the license is renewed. Signing out, returning badges, the emergency roll call, the admin screen and exporting your records keep working, and **we never delete your records**.
- **Early pause:** we may also pause a license early if a payment is reversed or a check is returned.
- **Restoring it:** a renewed license turns the device straight back on.

## 7. Your data

- **Where it's kept:** visitor records, photos, signatures and face data are kept on your tablet or in your own Google account (Google Sheets and Google Drive), depending on how you set up the kiosk. We don't receive, store or have access to them.
- **What the kiosk sends us:** to check its license, the kiosk downloads a small file from our website a few times a day. That request includes only the license's ID and the standard information any web request carries, such as an IP address.
- **Your responsibility:** you decide what the kiosk collects, how long records are kept (Admin → Settings), and how they're backed up.

## 8. Your responsibilities

- **Use it lawfully.** This includes privacy, data protection and anti-discrimination laws, and any rules for screening questions.
- **Tell visitors** how their information is used, for example with a notice by the kiosk. A template comes with the software (`docs/privacy-notice.md`).
- **Get consent where the law requires it.** This applies especially to photos, signatures and face recognition. Some states, such as Illinois, Texas and Washington, have specific biometric privacy laws.
- **Keep your admin PIN and any connection links private.**
