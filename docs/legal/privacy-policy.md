## Visitor information: we don't have it

The kiosk keeps visitor records on the customer's tablet or in the customer's own Google account. Those records include names, answers, photos, signatures and face data. We don't receive, store or have access to them. Questions about visitor information go to the business the visitor signed in with.

## What we collect about our customers

- **Contact and business details:** business name, contact name, email address, phone number, mailing address.
- **Orders and payments:** licenses bought, tablet and QR codes, amounts, dates, and check numbers or card payment references. We don't see or store card numbers: card payments are handled by our payment processor ([Stripe]).
- **License checks:** when a kiosk checks its license, our website host records the request in its logs. That's the license ID, the time, and the IP address and browser details any web request includes.
- **Support messages:** what you send us when you ask for help.

## Why

- To sell and renew licenses and send invoices and receipts.
- To provide updates and support.
- To keep accounting records and meet our legal and tax obligations.
- To protect against fraud and misuse of licenses.
