      Create your admin login

      Where should visitor records be kept?

      Pick what suits your business. Both are free, and visitors see the same kiosk either way.

      This app was built already connected to your Google Sheet, so records go there.

      Connect your Google Sheet

      You add a small script to a Google Sheet that you own. It takes about 5 minutes, once. Tip: this step is easier on a computer. Open the kiosk there, and tablets connected to the same sheet pick up these settings automatically.

      1. 1
        Create a Google Sheet in your Google account and name it, for example "Visitor log". It's private to you.
      2. 2
        Add the script. In the sheet, choose Extensions → Apps Script, delete the code that's there, paste the kiosk script and click Save.
      3. 3
        Deploy it. Click Deploy → New deployment, click the gear and choose Web app. Set Execute as to Me and Who has access to Anyone, then click Deploy.
      4. 4
        Allow access. Choose your account, then Advanced → Go to … (unsafe) → Allow. Google shows this warning for every script you add yourself. The script can only reach this sheet, a photo folder in your Drive, and send the alert emails you choose.
      5. 5
        Paste the Web app URL (it ends in /exec) below and tap Connect.

      Do you want to ask visitors extra questions?

      Screening questions come after the visitor's name and visit details, one per screen. Depending on the answer, the kiosk can politely decline a visitor, or let them in and alert your staff.

      Add a ready-made question

        When someone is declined

        They see this message, and their photo, answers and the time are kept for your staff. An admin can still let them in, either on the kiosk ("Staff override") or later from the admin screen.

        Records kept on this tablet can't send emails. Declines and alerts show in the admin screen (Needs attention), and a small red dot appears next to the clock until someone reviews them. Choose Google Sheets storage to also get emails.

        Only ask what your business is required or entitled to ask. Questions about citizenship or health, for example, are usually only appropriate where a law or contract requires them, such as U.S. export-control rules (ITAR / EAR) for defense work.

        Who do visitors come to see?

        Add the people visitors usually ask for. Their names come up as visitors type, so nobody has to guess the spelling. With Google Sheets, each host also gets an email the moment their visitor signs in.

          Records kept on this tablet can't send emails, so hosts aren't notified. Their names are still suggested to visitors. Choose Google Sheets storage to email hosts.

            Older visits, visitors who haven't come back, their photos and face data are deleted automatically once a day. Badges and settings are kept. Choose what your policies require.
